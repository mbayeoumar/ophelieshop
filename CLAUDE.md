## Project

**ophelieshop** is a kids crafting platform centered around Ophélie's creations. It is designed for children worldwide to discover, share, and celebrate crafts.

### Core features

- **Craft gallery** — Ophélie publishes her own crafts; kids browse and get inspired
- **Community sharing** — kids upload and showcase their own crafts
- **Craft exchange** — kids can trade crafts with each other
- **Tagging** — crafts are tagged by type, material, difficulty, etc.
- **Contests** — periodic craft contests where the community votes; winner earns a free craft or gift
- **Tools & supplies shop** — some tools are free to try, others are available for purchase

### Audience

The primary users are children, so the UI must be age-appropriate: simple, colorful, encouraging, and safe. Any social or exchange features must be designed with child safety in mind (no direct messaging, moderated content, parental-friendly flows).
